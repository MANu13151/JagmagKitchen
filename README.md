# JAGMAG — Curated Kitchen & Luxury Banquets (Jaipur)

> **Where Warmth Meets Grand Celebration.**  
> An ultra-refined, high-hospitality single-page web experience for **JAGMAG**, located in prime C-Scheme, Jaipur.

## 🏆 Key Features

- **Single-Row Editorial Header**: Left brand emblem with Devanagari signature, center navigation links, and dual high-converting CTAs (*Inquire Banquet* in Copper & *Book a Table* in Black Forest).
- **Cinematic Full-Bleed Architectural Hero**: Layered vignette over the glowing chandelier hall with trust indicators.
- **Two Distinct Worlds Showcase**: Asymmetric editorial presentation of both indoor dining and tropical atrium experiences with capacity metadata.
- **Interactive Banquet & Private Event Estimator**: Sales hook calculator for host celebrations (Engagement, Milestone Birthdays, Corporate Dinners) with live guest sliders (25–150 Pax), Classic vs Royal package tier selection, and 1-click WhatsApp quote generation.
- **Curated Kitchen Menu**: Artisanal Pan-Asian sushi, Awadhi North Indian specialties (24-hr Dal Jagmag Makhani), wood-fired flatbreads, and botanical mocktails with realistic Jaipur fine-dining pricing.
- **Contactless Table QR Ordering Simulator**: Interactive table selection (Table 01 to Banquet VIP), item add/remove, and live kitchen order transmission.
- **Dining Table Reservation Modal & Hospitality Footer**: Direct reservation workflow with valet parking assistance, operational hours, and Google Maps integration.
